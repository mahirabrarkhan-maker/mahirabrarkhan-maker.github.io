# Engineering that answers to Bangladesh and to the world.

At SSE, students and faculty build robots, read medical images, rethink energy systems and teach machines to see. Four research groups and five new laboratories are taking that work further.

[Explore our research](#research)[See what’s happening](#events)

[Featured story Five new labs, one purpose: more to build with High-performance computing, digital forensics, biomedical, energy and semiconductor labs are coming to SSE, each tied to a research group. Meet the new labs](#labs)

## Research groups

[All research](#research)

Faculty and students work together in four groups. Each group connects people, labs and projects around one question.

### Energy Research Group

Power systems, renewable generation and efficient use of energy, with a home in the planned Energy Lab.

Lab: Energy Lab (opening soon)

### Biomedical Research Group

Engineering for health: signals, imaging and devices that help clinicians and patients.

Lab: Biomedical Lab (opening soon)

### Robotics Research Group

Mobile robots, autonomy and mechatronics, built on student projects such as SLAM on a TurtleBot and robotics outreach through IITIR.

Linked to: IITIR, CIUAIC

### Vision Research Group

Computer vision and image understanding, from recognition to medical and scene analysis.

Lab: High Performance Computing Lab (opening soon)
